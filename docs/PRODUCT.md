# Daily Tracker Pro - Product Brief

## Vision

Daily Tracker Pro is a practical planning app for people who need simple daily discipline and lightweight project execution in one place.

The app should stay fast, local-first, and easy to understand. It is not meant to become a heavy project management suite. The strongest product angle is: daily habits, one-off tasks, and real-world project steps live in the same timeline.

## Target Users

- Solo builders and tradespeople planning jobs across days or months.
- Site managers coordinating concrete next steps, checks, drivers, material delivery, and inspection visits.
- Students or workers who need recurring routines plus one-time tasks.
- People who want progress motivation through streaks and weekly recaps.

## Core Jobs To Be Done

- I want to set recurring routines once and have them appear every week.
- I want to add a one-time task for a future day without retyping the whole week.
- I want to plan a project from one date to another and break it into concrete daily steps.
- I want to see what I should do today.
- I want to see how my week is going without being punished for future days that have not happened yet.
- I want reminders, but I do not want to manage a complicated calendar.

## Product Principles

- Keep planning concrete: every item should resolve to a day.
- Keep the UI calm and functional before making it pretty.
- Avoid enterprise complexity until real users ask for it repeatedly.
- Prefer local-first privacy and simple export over account systems in the early version.
- Paid features should save time or reduce operational mistakes, not lock basic daily usage.

## Current Product Surfaces

- Today: daily checklist and progress.
- Week: Sunday-to-Saturday view with quick add per day.
- Tasks: recurring tasks and one-time tasks.
- Plan: date-range projects with project steps on calendar days.
- Recap: week-to-date progress and Sunday previous-week review.
- Settings: language, theme, and notification permission.

## Open Product Questions

- Should professional users need project export as PDF/CSV?
- Should reminders become more reliable through a native app before adding paid plans?
- Should project templates exist for construction workflows such as bathroom renovation, flooring, demolition, delivery, and inspections?
- Should shared projects exist, or is this intentionally a solo planning tool?
